Privacy Policy

Data Alliance — Last updated: July 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Data Alliance ("we," "our," or "us") operates a content management application that integrates with third-party platforms including TikTok. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to our application, we may access:

  • Your public profile information (display name, username, avatar)
  • Account statistics (follower count, video count)
  • Videos you have published (metadata and engagement metrics)
  • Content you upload through our application

We do not collect or store your TikTok password.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we access to:

  • Upload and publish video and photo content to your TikTok account on your behalf
  • Display your published content and engagement metrics within our application
  • Monitor the status of content uploads

4. Data Storage and Security

  • OAuth tokens are stored in encrypted form using AWS Secrets Manager
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties
  • We retain your data only for as long as your account is connected to our application

5. Third-Party Services

Our application interacts with:

  • TikTok (Content Posting API, Display API) — governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Web Services — for secure credential storage

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Disconnect your TikTok account from our application
  • Request deletion of any data we hold about you
  • Revoke our access via TikTok's app authorization settings

7. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@mock-data-alliance.com. We will remove all stored tokens and cached data within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
