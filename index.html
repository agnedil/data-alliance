Data Alliance — Content Manager

Data Alliance Content Manager is an internal tool for scheduling and publishing brand content to social media platforms. It enables marketing teams to manage video and photo content from a single interface.

Features

  • Upload and publish video content to TikTok
  • Post photo slideshows with automatic music
  • Track publish status and engagement metrics
  • Manage content across multiple creator accounts

Supported Platforms

  • TikTok (Content Posting API + Display API)
